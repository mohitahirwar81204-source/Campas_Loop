TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

How recommendations are scored

Only eligible products at or below the budget are ranked. Each factor becomes a score from 0 to 100, then the selected priority applies its weights.

Final score = price weight × affordability + age weight × age score + condition weight × condition score
01

Affordability

100 × (1 − price ÷ budget). A lower eligible price scores higher.

02

Age

100 × (1 − age ÷ 10). A newer product scores higher.

03

Condition

Excellent 100, Good 80, Average 60, Fair 35, Poor 15.

Missing values: neutral score of 50 for age or condition.

Identical scores: condition, lower price, younger age, then listing ID.

Price flags: more than 35% from category median, only with 3+ comparable items.
